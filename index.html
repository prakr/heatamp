Technology

Heat-driven sorption. No moving parts.

HeatAmp uses chemisorption — a thermal compressor that stores energy in a salt matrix and releases it as heat on demand. No electrical upgrade. No compressor maintenance. Hermetically sealed for the long haul.

Hermetically sealed heat pump module. No moving parts. The batch process repeats every cycle — burner heat drives the chemistry on the way in, useful hot water comes out on the way back.

Market

A $2.7B replacement market — looking for a winner.

4 million gas water heaters are replaced annually in North America. The high-efficiency segment is just 5% today — but tightening regulation is projected to grow it to 40%. HeatAmp is positioned to claim the cost-effective end of that growth.
